Bali, Indonesia


Bali is a beautiful island in Indonesia known for its beaches, culture, temples, and scenic landscapes.

  1. Uluwatu Temple - A famous cliffside temple.
  2. Tegallalang Rice Terraces - Stunning green rice fields.
  3. Mount Batur - Popular for sunrise trekking.
  4. Seminyak Beach - Known for its beautiful sunsets.
  5. Ubud Monkey Forest - Home to many monkeys.
  • Nasi Goreng - Traditional Indonesian fried rice.
  • Satay - Grilled meat skewers with peanut sauce.
  • Babi Guling - Famous Balinese roast pork.
  • Mie Goreng - Delicious fried noodles.
  • Pisang Goreng - Fried banana snack.
